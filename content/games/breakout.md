A Breakout clone, my first game, written in Go with Ebitengine as a learning exercise. Move the paddle, keep the ball in play and break the bricks. [Play it full screen](https://breakout.markpoko.dev/).

Controls: A and D move the paddle, Space starts a game, F11 toggles fullscreen. Click the game first so it receives keyboard input.
