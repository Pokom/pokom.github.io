Proving Value in the Agentic Era

A recent article came out from Uber where they burned through a years worth of budget in the first four months of the year. The associated hackernews thread on is quite worthwhile to read through. Agents, and agentic coding, has proven to be more divisive in 6 months than k8s has in almost a decade of it’s existence. Proponents of it think the value is self explanatory and requires no further proof. Nay sayers claim not only is the spend on tokens shameful, the resulting work requires 10x the time to clean up. Like most things, the truth is somewhere between the two. But there’s something really important that’s worthwhile considering: Could you prove today that agents have provided value in your day to day life?
